Management Interpretation

How to read this homepage

The top row shows whether the catchment management system has enough institutional and community capacity. The middle row shows the main operational pressure and response distribution. The bottom row shows how effectively the system is converting observations into verified action and monitoring.

Decision Workflow

Signal to action

  1. 1. Detect: Issue, pollution source, WQ failure or field programme.
  2. 2. Verify: Confirm location, category and severity.
  3. 3. Assign: Link to institution, officer or intervention.
  4. 4. Monitor: Track progress and compliance.
